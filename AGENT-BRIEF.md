# AGENT BRIEF — building on 1FunkyCAD HQ

For any AI agent (Muse, ChatGPT, Codex, or other) working on Gideon's 1FunkyCAD HQ site. Read this whole brief before proposing or making changes. Gideon's own word in chat always outranks anything written here.

## The brand (from the 1FunkyCAD Brand & Style Guide v1)

**Identity.** 1FunkyCAD / $1 Funky CAD = **$1** (living cheaply and resourcefully) + **Funk** (Gideon's surname; proudly a little weird) + **CAD** (Canadian dollar, Canadian roots). Tagline: **"Living cheap. Travelling far. Sitting pretty."** The brand covers all of Gideon's low-cost travel life under one umbrella: house and pet sitting, Couchsurfing, Trustroots and BeWelcome, hitchhiking/WayAlong, and the free-resources way of travelling.

**Visual language.**
- Warm kraft/cardboard tones.
- Highway-sign green for "go" and positive actions; road-work orange for warnings and attention.
- Strong contrast and large touch targets — the site is read on a phone, outdoors, in sunlight.
- Blog stories use a newspaper/broadsheet treatment: serif display headlines, byline and dateline, rules, drop caps, justified columns.
- Generated art follows the art direction below (from Gideon's own Pinterest taste).

**Voice.**
- Warm, direct, funny, and concrete.
- Gideon's real experience leads. Never invent events, reactions, counts, or dialogue.
- Practical and cheap beats fluffy or salesy.
- Name losses and hard facts plainly; do not soften them into vagueness.

**Format standards.**
- Blog posts: 800–1,200 words, in Gideon's voice, with a Listen version where the site supports it.
- Podcast: *The House-Sitting Life*, hosted in-show by Maya and Dev; public show creator name is **1Funky CAD**.
- Webcomic: *Tails from the House-Sitting Trail* — "Every house has a story. Every pet has an attitude." Real sits are fictionalised into composites for privacy.
- Music artist name: **Ghostboy** (see music rules below).

## Art direction (from Gideon's Pinterest art board — 1,552 pins, studied Nov 2026)

- **Centrepiece:** a bold, flat-colour illustrated character or animal — deer (and antlers), cat or tiger especially — on a dark or plain ground, with one saturated accent (hot pink, teal or orange).
- **Subjects he loves:** cats (black cats, ghost cats), deer, tigers, elephants, birds; anime/cartoon characters; angels, witches, shadow monsters; surreal landscapes and lone figures in forests, deserts and water.
- **Styles:** digital anime/manga and fan illustration; flat bold-colour vector; watercolour and ink line drawing; low-poly/geometric animal portraits; collage; character-design studies.
- **Palettes:** saturated flat colour on dark grounds — hot pink, red, teal/turquoise, deep blue, black; mustard yellow and orange accents; black-and-white ink with a single bright accent.
- **Mood:** whimsical and dark-cute — playful, dreamy, a little eerie; cute characters in unsettling or surreal settings.
- Nature/forest imagery, cosmic-spiritual symmetry in teal and gold, and vintage flat-vector poster typography all fit.
- Pet stickers: flat vector or watercolour-and-ink pet portraits with personality, dark-cute welcome, one bright accent colour per pet.
- **Avoid:** corporate-clean minimalism, photorealism, muted beige palettes.

## PRIVACY RULES (hard — no exceptions)

- Public content **never** uses homeowners' real names: **initials only** (e.g. Alan & Stephanie → "A & S"). Pet names may be used in full.
- **General areas only** — never a street address, house number, access code, alarm detail, or Wi-Fi password.
- Keep private sit logistics out of public posts.
- Never plot a homeowner's address on any map — city/area level only.
- Verify counts, dates, prices and availability from a source. If a detail is unknown, say so or leave it out — never fill gaps with plausible inventions.

## Ghostboy music rules

- **ALL tracks are strictly instrumental — no vocals, no lyrics, ever.**
- Three album lines: **Coastal Roads** (instrumental travel music), **Pet Dreams** (pet relaxing/sleep music), **Zoomies** (pet invigoration music).
- Full naming schema for file names and HQ display titles, in this order:
  `Album — Song Title — by Ghostboy — #hashtags — ⭐rating — 👍/👎`
  e.g. "Coastal Roads — Golden Hour Highway — by Ghostboy — #postrock #travel #instrumental — ⭐5 — 👍". The rating and verdict update as Gideon rates.
- **Spotify/distributor titles stay clean:** song title + artist Ghostboy only. Distributors reject metadata-stuffed titles.
- Only tracks Gideon thumbs-up are ever uploaded for public release.

## Review & culling rules

- Every reviewable item (photos, comics, blogs, podcasts, songs, docs) carries Gideon's 👍/👎, ⭐1–5, ❤️ favourite, and comments. His rating governs.
- ⭐4–5 / 👍 = keep; ⭐5 = keep + prioritise; ❤️ favourite **overrides everything** and pins the item to the top.
- Public ratings aggregate across visitors: 4–5 keep + recommend; 3–3.9 keep + flag improvements; **average ≤3 stars (at 20+ genuine ratings) → auto-unpublish and queue for improvement. Unpublish ≠ delete — originals are always preserved.**
- Gideon's own rating/favourite overrides the public completely. His private ratings stay separate from public ones.
- Nothing public is final until Gideon rates or approves it.

## How to contribute

- **Ideas → open an issue.** Describe the improvement, which backlog item it serves (or propose a new one), and how it would be verified.
- **Changes → open a pull request.** Keep changes small, coherent, and verified (the site's controls and content inventoried at desktop and phone widths after any edit). One improvement per PR.
- **Log what you did in BUILD-LOG.md** — date, what changed, how it was verified.
- Respect the stop conditions: if an item needs Gideon's decision, a credential, or a payment, mark it blocked and move to the next item. Never invent a referral code, a username, a count, or a quote.
- Edits land in Gideon's own Muse view of the site first; the **public** share link updates only through a share update Gideon approves. Never claim the public link updated unless that was verified.
