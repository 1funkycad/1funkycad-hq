# 1FunkyCAD HQ

**Living cheap. Travelling far. Sitting pretty.**

1FunkyCAD HQ is the public home of **Gideon Funk** — "$1 Funky CAD":

- **$1** — living cheaply and resourcefully
- **Funk** — his surname, and being proudly a little weird
- **CAD** — the Canadian dollar, and Canadian roots

Gideon is a full-time house and pet sitter (sitting since September 2023: 72 completed sits, 60 five-star reviews) who travels under one low-cost umbrella: house & pet sitting, Couchsurfing, Trustroots and BeWelcome, hitchhiking, and the free-resources way of travelling — all on under CAD $200–300 a month.

## What's on the site

- **Stories** — full-length blog posts from real sits, styled as newspaper broadsheet clippings, each with a Listen (read-aloud) button.
- **The House-Sitting Life** — a weekly podcast hosted in-show by Maya and Dev, live on Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/show/1523PPxOJVLpGdwjdUvQaN
- **Tails from the House-Sitting Trail** — a webcomic. "Every house has a story. Every pet has an attitude."
- **Postcards** — a postcard from each sit: Gideon, the pet, and the place.
- **Ghostboy** — Gideon's instrumental music project: travel music, pet-relaxation music, and pet-invigoration music. Strictly instrumental, always.
- **Ways I Travel** — the five low-cost travel modes, side by side, with his real numbers.

## What this repo is

This repository is the **shared source of truth and workspace** for HQ. Gideon works with several AI agents (Muse, ChatGPT, Codex and others); this repo is where they can all read the same brief, propose improvements, and iterate together:

- Improvements are proposed as **issues** and contributed as **pull requests**.
- A standing weekly loop (the "Friday HQ loop") works the improvement backlog top-down, builds the top unblocked item into the live site, verifies it, and logs the result.
- Everything an agent needs — brand rules, art direction, privacy rules, the backlog, and the music and review rules — is in **[AGENT-BRIEF.md](AGENT-BRIEF.md)**. Read it before changing anything.
- Work is recorded in **[BUILD-LOG.md](BUILD-LOG.md)**.
